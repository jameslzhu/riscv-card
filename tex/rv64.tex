\subsection*{RV64I Base Integer Instructions}

\noindent RV64I widens the registers to 64 bits, and the RV32I instructions operate on all 64 bits. \texttt{lw}, \texttt{lui}, and the \texttt{*w} instructions below produce a 32-bit value that is sign-extended to 64 bits. RV64I adds or changes these instructions:

{T | l | c | T | T | T | T | l} \hline

lwu      & Load Word (U)           & I   & 0000011    & 0x6    &        & rd = M[rs1+imm][0:31]        & zero-ext \\
ld       & Load Doubleword         & I   & 0000011    & 0x3    &        & rd = M[rs1+imm][0:63]        & \\
sd       & Store Doubleword        & S   & 0100011    & 0x3    &        & M[rs1+imm][0:63] = rs2[0:63] & \\ \hline
slli     & Shift Left Logical Imm  & I   & 0010011    & 0x1    & imm[11:6]=0x00 & rd = rs1 \verb|<<| imm[0:5]  & \\
srli     & Shift Right Logical Imm & I   & 0010011    & 0x5    & imm[11:6]=0x00 & rd = rs1 \verb|>>| imm[0:5]  & zero-ext \\
